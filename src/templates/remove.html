How removal works

  • The hub hands out a nonce that expires after 15 minutes and works once.
  • You sign {action, siteUrl, did, nonce, createdAt} with your Ed25519 key.
  • The hub verifies that signature against your published key before deleting.
  • Your private key never leaves your machine.
